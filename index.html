I’m Alex, a student interested in designing games and building interactive experiences. I’m fascinated by the way a simple mechanic, a well-paced level, or a thoughtful visual detail can make players feel something real.

I’m currently developing my skills across game systems, level design, storytelling, and creative problem-solving. I enjoy learning how ideas move from a rough sketch to something people can play, share, and remember.

I’m building this portfolio as I grow and am excited to connect with studios and teams where I can contribute, learn from experienced creators, and help make games that invite people into new worlds.
